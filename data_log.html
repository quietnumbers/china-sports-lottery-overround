How this page is made

Every match with both the lottery’s three-way fixed odds and the European average close is loaded, the overround (sum of implied probabilities) is computed, and the daily means are aggregated. The page is rebuilt automatically (script build_data_log.py, stdlib only) and committed to the repository. Analysis, not betting advice.
